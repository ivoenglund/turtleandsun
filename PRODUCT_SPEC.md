# Turtle and Sun — Physical Calendar Product Spec

_Last updated: 2026-06-19_

---

## The Product

A beautifully printed family wall calendar, generated from the customer's own contacts (birthdays, anniversaries, special occasions). Designed primarily for the fridge but works on any wall.

---

## Formats

| SKU | Size | For |
|-----|------|-----|
| Single | 50×70 cm | Single-door fridge or wall |
| Double pack | 25×70 cm × 2 | Double-door / French door fridge |

**Key insight:** Single and double pack are the same print job — one 50×70 sheet. The double pack is simply cut in half at the end. Identical production cost, small price premium for the double pack.

---

## Surface & Materials

- **Laminated glossy finish** — customer can write on it with whiteboard/dry-erase markers and wipe clean
- **Magnet compatible** — fridge magnets work through the paper and laminate (fridge door behind remains magnetic)
- **Repositionable sticker sheet included** — static cling or low-tack adhesive, peels off and repositions without residue

---

## The Three-Layer System

| Layer | Product | How it works |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| 1 — Permanent | Printed calendar | Pre-printed occasions from contacts. Never changes. |
| 2 — Semi-permanent | Repositionable sticker sheet | Printed name/date stickers. Peel, stick, reposition. |
| 3 — Temporary | Whiteboard surface | Write appointments with dry-erase marker. Wipe off. |

---

## Sticker Sheet Contents

1. **Occasion stickers** — auto-generated from customer contacts (birthday, anniversary, etc.)
2. **Multi-day event stickers** — spans 2–4 days wide, customer types custom text + picks color (e.g. "Golf weekend", "Paris trip")
3. **Blank write-on stickers** — colored rectangles, customer writes on with marker
4. **Mini repositionable notes** — sized to fit one calendar day cell, like mini Post-its

All stickers sized to match the calendar grid precisely.

## Multi-Year Ordering Model

- Customer orders 1–5 years at once
- All calendars printed and shipped in one box, one postage charge
- Occasions (birthdays, anniversaries) are fixed — future years pre-generated automatically from same contact data
- Discount increases with years ordered
- Optional "life events update" — reprint one year for small fee if something changes
